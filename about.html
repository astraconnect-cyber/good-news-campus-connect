About Good News Campus Connect

Good News Campus Connect is a student-focused platform designed to connect students with reliable services, skilled service providers, and job opportunities.

Our goal is to make it easier for students to find affordable and accessible services within and around their campus communities.

🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to create opportunities for students by connecting customers with talented student service providers and making everyday services easier to access.

🔗 How We Work

Customers can search for services and submit requests through our platform.

Service providers can register their skills and services. Provider applications may be reviewed before becoming active on the platform.

Our goal is to create a safer and more organized connection between customers and service providers.

🌱 Our Vision

We envision a campus community where students can easily access useful services, earn money from their skills, and discover opportunities that support their growth.
